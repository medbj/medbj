# Human work before public launch

MENA Psychology is the current working name. English is the launch language in the complete attached brief. The referenced earlier multilingual archive is not present in the workspace; do not treat that missing source as recovered or invent its contents. The current articles and media include samples. Engineering completion does not make them reviewed or ready for public publication.

Use `docs/LAUNCH.md` for the full launch checklist. Keep `npm run check:launch` failing while samples, unverified crisis entries, unresolved legal placeholders, or required setup remain. Do not invent facts to clear the gate.

- [ ] Confirm the final site name, domain, tagline, social profiles, and responsible operator. Update `src/site.config.ts`, the canonical `SITE_URL`, and host settings. If later multilingual work is intended, supply the actual source brief and arrange qualified translation and cultural review before enabling languages.
- [ ] Supply the real author's name and accurate bio, or retain the permitted neutral “Editorial team” attribution without qualifications. Confirm who wrote each article. Verify any qualification before displaying it; never imply clinical review that did not happen.
- [ ] Write and human-review at least eight real articles, with plain language, checked references, suitable takeaways, and accurate dates. Decide a publishing cadence. Replace or remove all sample post and media records, including media that is disabled; do not just flip a sample flag on unreviewed copy.
- [ ] Confirm the real editorial workflow: writers, editors, source checks, handling of sensitive subjects, approval responsibility, conflicts, corrections, and review schedule. Replace the marked editorial-process placeholder with that actual process. Set `EDITORIAL_PROCESS_CONFIRMED=true` only after this work is done; it is a human attestation, not evidence supplied by the software. Record `reviewedOn` only when the stated review happened; an update date does not count as a review.
- [ ] Review content against `/content-guidelines`: no methods or enabling details for self-harm or suicide, no diagnosing or blaming readers, no sensational imagery, and no claims about unproven treatments, supplements, or “cures.” Confirm sensitive flags and crisis banners where appropriate.
- [ ] Independently verify each of the four prefilled crisis-resource records against current authoritative local sources. Check the country coverage, service name, phone number, hours, and languages. Set `verified: true` and an actual ISO `lastVerified` date only after that human check. Leave unverified contact details out of actionable UI. Do not add other phone numbers without verified source information; assign a person to keep records current.
- [ ] Check the two global support-directory links and the urgent-support wording. Confirm that the site does not imply a monitored inbox, emergency response, live support, or guaranteed service availability.
- [ ] Choose a newsletter provider and supply its public HTTPS form endpoint in `PUBLIC_NEWSLETTER_ACTION_URL`. Confirm email, honeypot, mandatory hidden fields, consent text, and its privacy notice. Use no API keys in public variables. Enable double opt-in, then test invalid email, a valid signup, confirmation, duplicate submission, bots, provider failure, unsubscribe, and JavaScript-disabled submission against the real provider. Verify the configured `PUBLIC_NEWSLETTER_EMAIL_FIELD` and `PUBLIC_NEWSLETTER_HONEYPOT_FIELD`; set `PUBLIC_NEWSLETTER_DOUBLE_OPT_IN_CONFIRMED=true` only after a human completes a real signup and confirms provider-side double opt-in.
- [ ] Supply working general, corrections, accessibility, and privacy contact routes with realistic response expectations. Do not request health information or identity documents through ordinary newsletter fields or an unverified email route.
- [ ] Have the Privacy Policy, Terms, Medical Disclaimer, editorial policy, and accessibility statement reviewed for the actual operator and worldwide audience. Confirm GDPR/UK GDPR and CCPA applicability and rights processes with an appropriate legal reviewer. Resolve all legal-review markers and set `LEGAL_REVIEW_CONFIRMED=true` only after the review is complete and the content matches reality. This value records a human attestation and does not itself perform or prove legal review.
- [ ] Confirm the controller's legal identity, host and newsletter providers, data uses and legal grounds, retention periods, processing locations and transfers, security controls, and privacy-request procedure. Check technical log retention and access; reading paths may be sensitive. Complete the current draft's missing operational details.
- [ ] Decide whether to enable cookieless analytics. Leave all analytics variables empty until the provider, data handling, retention, lawful basis, consent or opt-out needs, and privacy notice are checked. Verify configured scripts and network requests; “cookieless” does not by itself establish compliance.
- [ ] Supply genuine podcast and YouTube links, publication dates, durations, text descriptions, rights-cleared local thumbnail assets, captions, and accessible transcripts. Include a usable fallback link per item. Replace or remove both sample media records. Keep `features.media` false until ready, and confirm that a disabled or empty media collection creates no dead route or navigation link.
- [ ] Verify that no YouTube request occurs before a deliberate reader click, no media autoplays, and descriptions and fallback links remain available when an embed or platform is blocked.
- [ ] Keep advertising and affiliate features disabled for launch. Before any later commercial use, review the affiliate registry, disclosures, `rel="sponsored"`, sponsor policy, and privacy implications. Never monetize Get Help or sensitive articles, and never promote unproven treatments or “cures.”
- [ ] Check images and cover data for accurate required alt text, intrinsic dimensions, useful formats, and rights. Confirm the default branded social image and a real article's share preview. Do not use invented clinical credentials or performance statistics in any asset.
- [ ] Test at 375px and 1280px, at 200% zoom, by keyboard, with a screen reader, and on a real phone with a slow connection. Test long titles, empty topics, no search results, accent-insensitive search, JavaScript off, first and last pagination pages, drafts, duplicate slugs, and missing covers.
- [ ] Run `npm run test:guards`, automated accessibility checks, and Lighthouse. Record fresh results for the current full-brief implementation in `docs/QA.md`; earlier measurements must not be presented as current. Resolve issues before claiming the checklist target of at least 90 in mobile Performance, Accessibility, SEO, and Best Practices. Confirm article JavaScript stays under 20KB and measure mobile LCP against the 2.5-second budget. Automated checks do not establish complete WCAG conformance.
- [ ] Connect the final domain, enable HTTPS, choose one canonical hostname, and configure the www/non-www redirect. Verify canonical URLs, structured data, RSS, sitemap, robots, and the HTTP 404 response on production. Check one article with the Rich Results Test and submit the sitemap to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools using the owner's accounts.
- [ ] Select production access and host, push the repository to an authorized remote, and keep secrets outside Git. Set ownership for backups, security patches, retention, editorial maintenance, and crisis re-verification. Preserve a rollback release and confirm the production launch gate passes before public deployment.

- [ ] If the provider supports custom success/error redirects, configure and verify `/newsletter/confirm/` and `/newsletter/problem/`. These pages offer guidance and do not establish subscription or email-delivery status.
